## Findings

1. **Registration defect:** the skeleton is vertically reflected relative to the
   upright camera preview. In stationary views, fingertip labels appear over the
   back of the hand and the wrist/palm end appears near the fingertips. This is
   consistent across separated samples, rather than only a fast-motion lag.
   Source inspection finds a top-left capture-device conversion being reused for
   lower-left overlay paths. Fix this display transform before tuning Vision.
2. **Usable baseline view:** the fixed low/oblique angle shows one right-hand
   estimate and the visible finger motions clearly enough for a corrected-overlay
   retest. Foreshortening and occlusion remain risks. The recording does not
   establish simultaneous two-hand tracking, verified contact, or all-contact
   performance. Do not force a camera move before testing the registration fix.
3. **Confidence is not positional accuracy:** sampled central hand poses show
   about 20–21 visible joints at cutoff 0.72 and mean returned-joint confidence
   around 0.84–0.85. At 30 seconds a low-confidence pose estimate remains while
   zero joints meet the cutoff. Report raw pose estimates separately from poses
   with visible joints; do not call the latter a verified playing hand.
4. **Latency risk:** converted frame presentation timestamps are already about
   53 ms old on callback in these samples, before ~10 ms Vision work. This points
   to a camera/capture timing bottleneck to investigate. PTS age is not proven
   physical exposure/transport time and cannot establish contact-to-sound latency.
5. **Resource baseline:** displayed app CPU is about 0.42–0.45 of one core and
   physical footprint about 80 MiB, with nominal thermal state. The last observed
   memory-pressure event is Warning; that is a system event, not proof this app
   caused memory pressure or of current system-wide pressure.

## Corrections and next bounded experiment

- Project Vision's lower-left coordinates directly into the actual preview image
  rectangle; handle preview mirroring once and retain letterbox offsets. Add
  deterministic corner, wrist/tip, mirror, resize/round-trip and invalid-click tests.
- Remember the user's overlay cutoff (new installs default to 0.72). Preserve raw
  observations; do not smooth or rewrite confidence to conceal model errors.
- Keep 1280×720/30 fps as baseline. Owner clarified that resolution reduction is
  only a suggestion if evidence warrants it; accuracy, temporal sampling and
  latency take priority. Current load/drop counters do not justify a reduction.
  A finger-label visibility switch reduces visual clutter without changing
  inference/capture. No new resolution shortcut, FPS cap or smoothing is added.
- Proceed with a session-only four-corner playing-area calibration foundation.
  Its perspective transform describes on-plane projected positions only; it does
  not measure height or contact. Invalidate on capture changes/stalls and require
  reset after camera movement. No strike events or MIDI until evidence justifies
  the next step.
